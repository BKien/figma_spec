# Context

## Package boundary

Customer ecommerce interactions from account registration through catalog, checkout, account services, support, and blog reading.

## Canonical specification terms

- **Actor:** The visitor, customer, learner, instructor, candidate, or other user identified by an individual supplied use case.
- **Client:** The browser or application surface that presents the recorded interaction.
- **System:** The product service that returns the recorded outcome.
- **Use-case command:** A normalized representation of one submitted interaction.
- **Use-case result:** The recorded outcome correlated to the actor and request.
- **Source specification:** An original attached Markdown document preserved under `source/`.

Domain-specific terms, field names, statuses, and exact messages retain the spelling and meaning defined in each source specification.
